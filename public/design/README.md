# Design files

The raw art from the jam, before any of it got turned into a card. The game doesn't load anything in here, I just didn't want to lose the originals.

- `frames/`: the blank card frames (characters, spells and equipment, dark arts, roles)
- `characters/`: the five character portraits (1 Lord Bacon, 2 Lilium, 3 Tiny, 4 Professor Mittens, 5 Ember)
- `roles/`: the role portraits
- `spells/`: spell and equipment pictures
- `ui/`: the button sheets (the sword, shield and rook icons and the wooden plaque were cut from these) and a chest we never used

Cards were made by pasting a 42x40 picture at (5, 16) for spells or (6, 16) for characters and roles, trimmed to the inside of the picture window so the black outline stays on top.
